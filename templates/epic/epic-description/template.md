# EPIC NAME

A high-level initiative that groups related features toward a strategic business or product goal.

## Format

* **Title:** {short, verb-noun phrase, e.g. "Enable Multi-Tenant Support"}
* **Goal:** {one sentence business or product objective}
* **Scope:** {what is in scope — key capability areas}
* **Out of Scope:** {explicit exclusions to avoid scope creep}
* **Success Metrics:** {measurable outcomes, e.g. KPIs, OKRs}
* **Dependencies:** {upstream epics, external teams, or systems}
* **Timeline / Target Quarter:** {Q{n} {year}}

### Example

* **Title:** Enable Multi-Tenant Support
* **Goal:** Allow enterprise customers to isolate their data within a shared platform instance
* **Scope:** Tenant provisioning, data partitioning, per-tenant configuration
* **Out of Scope:** Billing per tenant, custom domains
* **Success Metrics:** 5 enterprise pilots onboarded by Q3; zero data leakage incidents
* **Dependencies:** Auth Epic (E-12), Infrastructure team
* **Timeline / Target Quarter:** Q2 2026

## Leading Questions

* What business problem or strategic goal does this epic address?
* How will we measure success — what does "done" look like at the epic level?
* What is explicitly out of scope to keep the epic focused?
* Which teams, systems, or epics must be completed or coordinated before this can start?
* What is the target timeframe and are there hard deadlines?
